Topic 12: Drinking Water Suitability Standards

Calculated per BIS 10500:2012. Safe Drinking Area: GWQI < 100, F ≤ 1.0 mg/L, NO3 ≤ 45 mg/L, TDS ≤ 1000 mg/L. Marginal Area: GWQI 100–150, F ≤ 1.5 mg/L, TDS ≤ 2000 mg/L (usable with standard treatment). Unsuitable Area: Critical fluoride or salinity hazard requiring RO or surface water substitution.

Topic 13: Agricultural & Irrigation Cropland Standards

Calculated per US Salinity Laboratory (USSL) and FAO 29. Prime Cropland: SAR < 10, EC < 1,500 μS/cm, RSC < 1.25 meq/L (ideal for paddy, sugarcane, banana, vegetables). Moderate / Salt-Tolerant: SAR 10–18, EC 1,500–3,000 μS/cm (ideal for cotton, millets, pulses). Sodicity Hazard: High SAR causing soil infiltration breakdown.

Topic 11: Multi-Year ML Predictive Modeling

Calibrated Theil-Sen multi-variate trend regression with aquifer inertia constraints (maximum ±3.5% annual drift based on hydrogeological recharge dynamics). Evaluates post-2026, 2028, 2030, and 2035 horizons to enable proactive infrastructure planning for TWAD Board and PWD Water Resources Department.
